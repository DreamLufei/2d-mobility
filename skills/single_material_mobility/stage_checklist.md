# Stage Checklist

- `prepare`: inputs exist, workdir ready, structure summary recorded
- `relax`: relaxed structure and reciprocal lattice available
- `scf`: Fermi level and CHGCAR available
- `band`: band edges and k-point metadata available
- `effective_mass`: carrier masses populated
- `strain_loop`: cumulative strain data complete enough for fitting
- `refinement`: accept, refine, reject, terminate, or escalate
- `mobility`: mobility, E1, and C2D populated
- `validation`: final decision and confidence recorded
- `report`: final summary and canonical outcome written
